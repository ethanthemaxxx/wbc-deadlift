DEADLIFT SKIES — CHECKING

 

 

Deadlift is an independent brand studio in Pittsburgh's Strip District. We believe every brand is a weight, and most are carried badly. We pick up the ones gone dead, find the grip, and lift. No tricks. Just good form, then loud.

Every job starts the same way. We walk the shop floor, meet the people, and hold the product. Then we strip the brand to the bar and add weight one plate at a time. If a plate does not help the lift, it goes back on the rack.
