About Deadlift

01 / THE SHOP

Deadlift is a small, independent studio in a Strip District warehouse that used to move fruit. Designers, a printer and a very old couch share one room. The coffee is bad. The work is not.

02 / THE METHOD

We start by finding out why the brand went dead. Usually it is not the logo. It is the story, the shelf, or the room. Then we fix that first and draw second.

03 / THE LIFTS

Identity, packaging, campaigns and motion. Four lifts, one crew. A bottle, a record sleeve, a poster on a brick wall and a thirty-second film should look like one family.

04 / THE CITY

Pittsburgh built bridges, rails and a great deal of steel. It taught us that a thing should work first, then look good doing it. We stay for the rivers, the hills and blunt neighbors.

05 / THE RULE

We only say yes to work we can finish properly. That means fewer clients, longer talks and no pitch decks full of maybe. If we take the job, we carry it to the end.
